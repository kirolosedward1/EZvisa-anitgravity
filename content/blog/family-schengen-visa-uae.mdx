Traveling to Europe with your family is an incredible experience, but it multiplies the complexity of the visa application process. 

When applying as a family unit from the UAE, embassies look closely at who is sponsoring whom. Here is everything you need to know to ensure your entire family gets approved together.

## 1. The Sponsor Concept

In a family application, there is usually one primary applicant (the sponsor) who provides the financial backing for the trip, and dependents (spouse, children, or domestic helpers) who rely on that sponsor.

The sponsor must provide their bank statements and employment documents (NOC) to prove they can afford the trip for everyone. 

## 2. Documents Required for a Sponsored Spouse

If a wife or husband is unemployed or a homemaker in the UAE, they must apply as a dependent. They will need:
* A **Sponsorship Letter** written and signed by the employed spouse, stating they will cover all travel, accommodation, and daily expenses.
* A copy of the sponsor’s passport and UAE visa.
* The sponsor’s original bank statements (3-6 months) and NOC.
* **Proof of Relationship:** An attested marriage certificate (translated into English if in another language).

## 3. Documents Required for Children

Minors (under 18) require specific documentation to prevent child trafficking:
* **Birth Certificate:** Attested and translated into English.
* **Consent Letter:** If the child is traveling with only one parent, the non-traveling parent must provide a notarized letter of consent along with a copy of their passport.
* Both parents must sign the child’s application form.

## 4. Applying for a Nanny or Maid

Many UAE families travel with domestic helpers. Embassies have very strict rules for this:
* The helper must have been employed by the sponsor in the UAE for at least one year.
* The sponsor must provide a specialized guarantee letter confirming they will cover all expenses and ensure the helper returns to the UAE.
* The helper must have their own application form, travel insurance, and dedicated appointment slot.

## 5. Group Appointments

Whenever possible, families should book appointments at the exact same time at VFS/TLS and submit their applications at the same counter. The consular officer evaluates the family as a single unit, and separating the files can lead to confusion or partial rejections.
