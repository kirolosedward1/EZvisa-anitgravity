Receiving a visa rejection letter is incredibly frustrating. Not only do you lose the non-refundable embassy fees, but a rejection remains in the Schengen Information System (VIS) and can negatively impact future applications.

Here are the 7 most common reasons Schengen embassies in the UAE reject visas, and how EZvisa ensures this doesn't happen to you.

## 1. Lack of Proof of Return (Weak Ties to the UAE)
The embassy needs to be convinced you will not overstay. If you have a short employment history, low salary, or weak family ties in the UAE, the consular officer may doubt your intention to return.
**The Fix:** A highly persuasive cover letter that aggressively highlights your stability in the UAE (long-term job, property, family).

## 2. Unverifiable Flight or Hotel Bookings
Many applicants use fake, photoshopped, or instantly cancelled flight tickets. Embassies actively verify PNR codes with airlines and hotels. If they find the booking doesn't exist, it is an automatic rejection for "unverifiable intent."
**The Fix:** EZvisa provides authentic, verifiable flight reservations and hotel bookings that hold up to embassy scrutiny.

## 3. Illogical or Missing Travel Itinerary
If you are visiting three countries in 7 days, or staying in a remote village for two weeks without a logical explanation, the embassy will suspect you are hiding your true intentions.
**The Fix:** We craft a highly logical, day-by-day travel itinerary that makes perfect sense to a visa officer.

## 4. Insufficient Financial Means
Your bank statement must show that you can comfortably afford the trip (flights, hotels, daily expenses) without depleting your life savings. Large, unexplained cash deposits just before applying are a major red flag.
**The Fix:** We review your profile and advise you on exactly how your bank statements should look before you apply.

## 5. Applying to the Wrong Embassy
If your itinerary shows you spending 5 days in Italy and 2 days in Spain, but you apply to the Spanish embassy, you will be rejected for applying to the wrong jurisdiction.
**The Fix:** EZvisa audits your intended travel plans and ensures the bookings and forms are perfectly aligned with the correct embassy's rules.

## 6. Invalid Travel Insurance
Insurance that does not cover the minimum €30,000, does not cover all Schengen member states, or does not cover repatriation will result in refusal.

## 7. Discrepancies in Documentation
If your NOC says you are traveling from the 10th to the 20th, but your flight bookings are for the 12th to the 22nd, the mismatch creates doubt and leads to rejection.
**The Fix:** EZvisa's rigorous quality control ensures every date, name, and detail matches perfectly across all 5 core documents we provide.
