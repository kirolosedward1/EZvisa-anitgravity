Many applicants look up the official Schengen visa fee—which is €90 (around 360 AED)—and assume that is the total cost of their application. 

Unfortunately, that is just the beginning. Securing a Schengen visa in the UAE involves several layers of mandatory fees. Furthermore, making a mistake in your application can cost you thousands of dirhams in lost bookings.

Here is the true breakdown of what a Schengen visa costs in 2026.

## 1. The Mandatory Processing Fees

When you attend your appointment, you will pay these non-refundable fees directly to the visa center:
* **The Embassy Fee:** €90 for adults (approx. 360 AED) and €45 for children aged 6-12 (approx. 180 AED). Children under 6 are free.
* **The VFS/TLS Service Fee:** Visa application centers charge an administration fee, which usually ranges from 100 AED to 150 AED depending on the country.
* **Courier & SMS (Optional but Common):** If you want your passport delivered back to your home, expect to pay an extra 40 to 60 AED.

**Base Cost:** Approximately 500 to 550 AED per adult.

## 2. Mandatory Travel Requirements

You cannot submit your application without purchasing certain items upfront:
* **Travel Medical Insurance:** A policy covering €30,000 for the duration of your trip usually costs between 50 to 150 AED, depending on your age and the length of the trip.
* **Flight and Hotel Bookings:** This is the dangerous part. If you book fully paid, non-refundable flights and hotels, you are risking thousands of dirhams if your visa is delayed or rejected. If you book fully refundable options, you pay a massive premium to the airlines and hotels upfront.

## 3. The Hidden Cost of Mistakes

If you try to save money by using fake flight tickets generated by shady online generators, the embassy will check the PNR code, find it invalid, and reject your visa. 

A rejection means:
* You lose the 500+ AED processing fees.
* You lose the insurance cost.
* You lose any non-refundable deposits on hotels.
* Your permanent Schengen record is stained, making future applications much harder.
