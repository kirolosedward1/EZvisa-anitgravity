If your goal is simply to visit Europe and you are flexible with your entry point, choosing the right embassy can significantly increase your chances of approval and drastically reduce your waiting time. 

Based on our experience preparing applications from the UAE, here are five Schengen countries with a straightforward application process in 2026. Official statistics are published by the European Commission: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen-borders-and-visa/visa-policy_en. Visa decisions are always made by the embassy or consulate.

## 1. Spain 🇪🇸

Spain consistently ranks as one of the most welcoming Schengen countries for tourists applying from the UAE. 
* **Consular Process:** Transparent submission process for applicants with stable UAE residency.
* **Processing Time:** Known for remarkable efficiency, often returning passports within 3 to 7 working days via BLS International.
* **Why we recommend it:** Generous with visa validity periods for frequent travelers.

## 2. France 🇫🇷

France remains the most visited country in the world and has a streamlined visa process to match.
* **Consular Process:** Clear requirements; a complete, consistent file avoids common mistakes.
* **Processing Time:** While appointment slots can fill up fast, the actual processing time after submission is usually swift (around 10-15 days).
* **Why we recommend it:** France is known to issue long-term multiple-entry visas (from 6 months up to 5 years) to applicants with a solid travel history.

## 3. Germany 🇩🇪

Germany is renowned for its structured evaluation process. Applicants with complete, verifiable paperwork benefit from a clear, predictable review.
* **Consular Process:** Clear, rule-based document requirements.
* **Processing Time:** Generally 10-15 working days.
* **Why travelers choose it:** Highly structured. If your documents are complete and verifiable, your application is processed systematically.

## 4. Switzerland 🇨🇭

Switzerland offers a highly organized application process through VFS Global in the UAE.
* **Consular Process:** Thorough review with focus on accommodation and financial solvency.
* **Processing Time:** Efficient, usually taking 7-12 working days.
* **Why travelers choose it:** Transparent documentation guidelines and reliable appointment handling.

## 5. Greece 🇬🇷

Greece actively encourages tourism from the Middle East and maintains an accessible consulate in the UAE.
* **Consular Process:** Clear tourist visa requirements, especially during peak holiday seasons.
* **Processing Time:** Around 10-15 working days.
* **Why travelers choose it:** Popular destination with straightforward travel itinerary requirements.
