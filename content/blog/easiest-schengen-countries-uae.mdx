If your goal is simply to visit Europe and you are flexible with your entry point, choosing the right embassy can significantly increase your chances of approval and drastically reduce your waiting time. 

Based on recent data and our extensive experience handling thousands of applications from the UAE, here are the top 5 easiest Schengen countries to get a visa from in 2026.

## 1. Spain 🇪🇸

Spain consistently ranks as one of the most welcoming Schengen countries for tourists applying from the UAE. 
* **Approval Rate:** Very high for applicants with stable UAE employment.
* **Processing Time:** Known for remarkable efficiency, often returning passports within 3 to 7 working days via BLS International.
* **Why we recommend it:** Generous with visa validity periods for frequent travelers.

## 2. France 🇫🇷

France remains the most visited country in the world and has a streamlined visa process to match.
* **Approval Rate:** Excellent, provided your documents are perfectly in order.
* **Processing Time:** While appointment slots can fill up fast, the actual processing time after submission is usually swift (around 10-15 days).
* **Why we recommend it:** France is known to issue long-term multiple-entry visas (from 6 months up to 5 years) to applicants with a solid travel history.

## 3. Germany 🇩🇪

Germany is renowned for its strict adherence to rules, which actually works in your favor. If you follow the checklist perfectly, approval is almost guaranteed.
* **Approval Rate:** High for UAE residents with flawless paperwork.
* **Processing Time:** Generally 10-15 days.
* **Why we recommend it:** Highly predictable. If your EZvisa file is properly prepared, you will get your visa.

## 4. Switzerland 🇨🇭

Switzerland offers a highly organized application process through VFS Global in the UAE.
* **Approval Rate:** Very favorable for UAE applicants who can demonstrate strong financial standing.
* **Processing Time:** Efficient, usually taking 7-12 working days.
* **Why we recommend it:** A great entry point for Central Europe with transparent requirements.

## 5. Greece 🇬🇷

Greece actively encourages tourism from the Middle East and maintains a very accommodating consulate in the UAE.
* **Approval Rate:** High, especially during the summer holiday season.
* **Processing Time:** Around 10-15 days.
* **Why we recommend it:** Less stringent financial requirements compared to Northern European countries.
