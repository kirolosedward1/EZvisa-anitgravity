The number one reason for Schengen visa delays and rejections is missing or incorrect documentation. Embassies in the UAE process hundreds of applications daily; they do not have time to ask for missing papers—they will simply reject your application.

Here is the ultimate document checklist to ensure your file is flawless.

## 1. Personal & Identification Documents

* **Original Passport:** Must be valid for at least 3 months beyond your intended return date from the Schengen Area. It must have at least two blank pages.
* **UAE Residence Visa / Emirates ID:** Must be valid for at least 3 months beyond your return date.
* **Passport Photos:** Two recent biometric passport photos (35x45mm) with a white background, taken within the last 6 months.

## 2. Professional & Financial Documents

* **No Objection Certificate (NOC):** An original, signed, and stamped letter from your UAE employer stating your job title, salary, joining date, and approved leave dates. (If self-employed, provide your Trade License and a self-drafted letter).
* **Bank Statements:** Original stamped bank statements for the last 3 to 6 months. They must show a regular salary credit and a healthy closing balance.
* **Payslips:** The last 3 months of payslips (optional but highly recommended if your bank statement doesn't clearly state "Salary").

## 3. Core Travel Documents (The EZvisa Specialty)

This is where applications often fall apart. Embassies demand proof that you have a planned trip and intend to return to the UAE.

* **Schengen Visa Application Form:** Fully completed and signed.
* **Travel Medical Insurance:** Must cover the entire Schengen area with a minimum coverage of €30,000 for medical emergencies and repatriation.
* **Round-Trip Flight Itinerary:** A verifiable flight reservation showing your entry and exit dates.
* **Proof of Accommodation:** Hotel bookings covering every single night of your stay in the Schengen Area.
* **Cover Letter:** A personalized letter explaining the purpose of your trip, your strong ties to the UAE, and your financial capability.
* **Day-by-Day Itinerary:** A logical, detailed plan of what you will do each day of your trip.
