#### 1. Scope
This policy applies to the EZvisa service fee: **AED 249 per application**, or **AED 498 for an applicant travelling with a spouse** (two applications). It does not apply to embassy/consulate visa fees, visa application centre (e.g. VFS Global) fees, insurance premiums or any other third-party charges, which are paid to those third parties and are governed by their own policies. EZvisa is not VAT-registered and no VAT is charged.

#### 2. The cut-off rule
There is one cut-off point: **the start of preparation**. Preparation starts when our team begins working on your file (reviewing your documents or drafting any part of your application). We will notify you in your account, by email or WhatsApp when preparation starts.

- **Before preparation starts:** you may cancel and receive a **full refund** of the service fee.
- **Once preparation has started, or once your documents have been prepared or submitted:** **no refund** is available.

#### 3. When EZvisa fails to deliver
Regardless of the cut-off, you will receive a **full refund** if EZvisa fails to deliver the service you paid for, for example if we do not deliver your prepared application file, or we cannot provide the service for the destination you selected. We will first offer to correct any error in documents we prepared, free of charge.

A **duplicate payment** for the same application is always refunded in full.

#### 4. What is not a reason for a refund
- Visa refusal, delay or any other decision by an embassy, consulate or visa centre. EZvisa prepares applications; it does not decide them and does not guarantee approval.
- Missed or unavailable appointments, changed travel plans or a change of mind after preparation has started.
- Refusals or delays caused by incomplete, inaccurate or non-genuine information or documents you provided.

#### 5. How to request a refund
Send your request with your application reference number to {{EMAIL}} or WhatsApp +971 54 710 9533. We will respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method via the payment gateway within 7 business days of approval; the time for funds to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

#### 6. Your statutory rights
Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under UAE Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on Consumer Protection or other mandatory law.
