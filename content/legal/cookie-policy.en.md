## 1. What cookies are
Cookies are small text files stored on your device. "First-party" cookies are set by ezvisa.net; "third-party" cookies are set by other providers. Local storage and app SDKs work in a similar way.

## 2. Categories

| Category | Purpose | Consent needed? |
|---|---|---|
| **Strictly necessary** | Login, security, saving your application progress, remembering your cookie choices, payment checkout | No — always active |
| **Functional** | Live chat and remembering language preference | Yes |
| **Analytics** | Understanding how visitors use the site (Google Analytics via Google Tag Manager) | Yes |
| **Marketing** | Measuring our advertising through Google Tag Manager | Yes |

Non-essential cookies are **not set until you consent** in the cookie banner.

## 4. Consent and withdrawal
- On your first visit, the banner lets you **Accept all**, **Reject** or **Manage** by category. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.
- You can change or withdraw consent at any time via **Cookie settings** in the website footer. Withdrawal applies from that moment and does not affect earlier processing.
- You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; blocking necessary cookies may stop parts of the site (e.g. login, checkout) from working.
- Opt out of Google Analytics across sites: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
- App push notifications can be turned off in your device settings.
- We ask for consent again after 12 months or when we add new non-essential cookies.
